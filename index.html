Responsibility split

Your robot is CE marked. Your installation is not.

Under EN ISO 3691-4:2023, the CE mark covers the driverless vehicle as a machine. It does not cover the site it drives on. Annex A of that standard places the preparation of the operating zone — floor condition, floor marking, code accuracy, rack geometry and residual-risk mitigation — on the party that installs the system, not on the manufacturer that shipped the robot.

That is a statement about the regulation, not a Hotmeer certification. The operating zone is precisely the scope our crews self-perform, which is why we publish where the line falls before anyone signs.
